AAAI-27 Bridge Program

Constraint Programming & Machine Learning

A bridge between the reasoning and learning communities: tutorials, hands-on labs and open discussion on how constraint-based reasoning and machine learning can strengthen each other.

February 16–17, 2027
Montréal, Canada · in person
Call for participation Program
About the bridge

Reasoning meets learning

Constraint programming offers declarative modelling, guarantees and explainability; machine learning offers the ability to learn from data at scale. The CPML Bridge brings both communities together at AAAI-27 to learn each other's tools, share open problems, and start collaborations that outlast the event.

Participants only need introductory familiarity with one of the two fields. Researchers from related areas, including SAT/SMT, operations research, combinatorial optimisation, planning and neuro-symbolic AI, are very welcome.

Education

Tutorials and software labs introducing the core ideas and tools of each discipline to the other.

Collaboration

Short talks, posters, panels and structured discussion on open questions at the CP/ML interface.

Community

Shared benchmarks, resources and a mailing list that keep the community connected beyond AAAI-27.

Topics include, but are not limited to

  • Learning to solve & learning heuristics
  • Constraint acquisition
  • Predict-then-optimise / decision-focused learning
  • Constraints in neural networks & training
  • Verification of ML models
  • LLMs for modelling & solving
  • Neuro-symbolic reasoning
  • Explainability & fairness via constraints
  • Algorithm selection & configuration
  • Benchmarks & datasets
  • Applications
