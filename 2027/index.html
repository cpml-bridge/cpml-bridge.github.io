About

The CPML Bridge brings together the constraint programming and machine learning communities at AAAI-27. Through tutorials, short talks and discussion, participants learn each other's tools, share open problems and start new collaborations. Only introductory familiarity with one of the two fields is needed; researchers from related areas (SAT, operations research, optimisation, neuro-symbolic AI) are welcome.

Topics include, but are not limited to:

  • Learning to solve
  • Learned heuristics
  • Algorithm selection
  • Constraint acquisition
  • Predict-then-optimise
  • Decision-focused learning
  • Constraints in neural networks
  • Verification of ML models
  • LLMs for modelling and solving
  • Benchmarks and applications

Call for participation

Participation is by invitation based on a short submission that combines CP and ML in some way and is accessible to non-specialists. We welcome research abstracts, position papers, open problems, and tool or demo descriptions.
